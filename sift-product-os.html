From launched to measurable to sellable — the version of this plan that the evidence actually supports.

Drafted 27 Jul 2026 · 10–15 hrs/week · one person · $0 acquisition budget

01 The thesis

The sellable asset is the dossier dataset and the audience relationship — not the aggregator.

The AI-summarization layer has the worst legal posture (Cohere, Meltwater), the worst SEO posture (Google's scaled-content-abuse and helpful-content policies describe it near-verbatim), the worst channel posture (r/politics bans aggregators; HN flags politics), and no verified unit economics anywhere in the category.

The dossiers are original research over public records — OpenSecrets, GovTrack, ProPublica, FEC, FARA. Owned, evergreen, long-tail-searchable, licensable, defensible.

Test every proposal against one question: does this shift weight from the summarizer to the dossiers?

04 What to measure

North star: weekly returning users who ran a comparison or opened a dossier. Not signups. Not pageviews.

EventWhy it exists
comparison_runThe activation event — not signup. Seeing how three outlets framed the same story is the moment the product delivers its promise.
dossier_viewedPlus entity_type. The differentiated, SEO-viable, licensable surface.
primer_expandedDoes anyone actually want "what you should know first"? Cheap to answer, expensive to assume.
topic_searchedIntent signal. What people search for is the roadmap.
digest_subscribedThe audience relationship — the asset that actually transfers in a sale.
returned_within_7dDerived. The only real early retention signal.
The honest caveat
There is no published news-vertical D1/D7/D30 or DAU/MAU benchmark — no analytics vendor breaks out news. If someone hands you one, ask for the source. Chartbeat's global average engaged time per pageview is 26 seconds; if Sift saves people time, low engaged time may be a success. Return frequency is the north star, not time on site.
Below a few hundred users
Cohort curves are noise. The correct instrument is a spreadsheet of named individuals who came back unprompted. Doing the power-user curve by hand at 10–50 users isn't a shortcut — it's the right method. Instrument now, read later.

07 Dates on the calendar

2 Aug 2026
EU AI Act Art. 50(4) applies. Deployers publishing AI-generated text on matters of public interest must disclose it — exempt where content has human review/editorial control and a named person holds editorial responsibility. The /about page is the fix. (Digital Omnibus delay: unverified.)
15 Sept 2026
Cloudflare default-blocks mixed-use crawlers on ad-bearing pages — new customers, new sites of existing customers, and all free-tier users; Pay Per Crawl becomes Pay Per Use. The most concrete near-term threat to ingest, independent of any lawsuit.
Ongoing
Content-rights posture. Cohere (MTD denied 14 Nov 2025) held AI substitutive summaries may plausibly infringe. Anthropic's indemnity excludes "Inputs provided by Customer" — the whole RAG pipeline. Summaries stay short and point to the source; never reconstruct the lede.
Ongoing
Dossier defamation. Starbuck v. Google (MTD denied 24 Jul 2026): disclaimers don't defeat the claim at the pleading stage. Bouck v. Meta: §230 doesn't cover AI-generated content. Every claim about a living person cites the primary record; never characterize a legal outcome beyond what the record literally says.
